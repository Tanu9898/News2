Manoj Gadarpuriya: The Rising Music Artist Building His Own Sound

The independent music scene is constantly introducing new artists who are bringing their own sound, energy, and creative identity to audiences. Manoj Gadarpuriya is one such name attracting attention through a growing catalogue of music and collaborations.

Known professionally as Manoj Gadarpuriya, the singer and music artist is developing his presence through original releases that explore contemporary sounds while maintaining a strong connection with the culture and attitude that define today's Punjabi music landscape.

Rather than being defined by a single release, Manoj Gadarpuriya's work is beginning to take shape as a broader musical catalogue, with tracks across different moods and concepts.

Manoj Gadarpuriya's Growing Music Catalogue

A major part of Manoj Gadarpuriya's artistic identity is his focus on releasing music consistently.

His catalogue includes songs such as "My City," "My State My Pride," "KUDI FIRE," and "Adat Ban Gai." His recent releases also include collaborations with other artists, showing his willingness to work across different creative directions.

"My City," released in July 2026, presents Manoj Gadarpuriya as the lead artist and reflects his continued focus on developing his individual musical identity.

Another notable release is "My State My Pride," a collaboration featuring Malwai Partap. The track was released in July 2026 and adds another dimension to his growing body of work.

Collaboration Is Becoming an Important Part of His Work

Collaboration has become an important element of modern independent music, and Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent releases demonstrate that approach.

His collaboration with Malwai Partap on "KUDI FIRE" brings together two artists on a contemporary track designed around a strong, energetic musical identity.

He has also worked with Harry Balachour on "Adat Ban Gai," further expanding the range of artists connected with his music.

These collaborations allow Manoj Gadarpuriya to explore different sounds while continuing to establish his own identity as a singer.

"My City" and the Sound of Manoj Gadarpuriya

Among his recent releases, "My City" is particularly significant because it is presented as a solo Manoj Gadarpuriya release.

Released in July 2026, the track gives listeners another opportunity to experience his work directly rather than through a collaborative project. The release is part of his continuing effort to expand his catalogue and strengthen his presence as an independent music artist.

For an emerging artist, individual releases such as "My City" are important because they help establish a recognizable identity beyond individual collaborations.

A New Phase of Musical Growth

Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent releases suggest that his music career is entering an increasingly active phase.

His catalogue is not limited to one particular style or one type of collaboration. From solo releases to projects with artists such as Malwai Partap and Harry Balachour, his work demonstrates an interest in exploring different creative possibilities.

That variety is becoming an important part of his identity.

In August 2026, Manoj Gadarpuriya was also featured on "Built Different," a collaboration with Malwai Partap. The release further adds to his recent run of musical projects.

Building a Name Through Music

For independent artists, building recognition is often a gradual process. Every song, collaboration, and release contributes to how audiences understand an artist.

For Manoj Gadarpuriya, the growing catalogue provides an increasingly clear picture of an artist focused on creating and releasing new music rather than being defined by a single project.

His recent activity across music platforms also gives listeners multiple entry points into his work—from solo tracks such as "My City" to collaborations including "My State My Pride," "KUDI FIRE," "Adat Ban Gai," and "Built Different."

What Comes Next for Manoj Gadarpuriya?

The next stage of Manoj Gadarpuriya's journey will be shaped by the music he continues to create and the collaborations he chooses to pursue.

With a growing catalogue and an active release schedule, he is steadily building a larger musical identity. As his audience discovers more of his work, each new release has the opportunity to introduce another side of his artistry.

For listeners looking for emerging independent music artists, Manoj Gadarpuriya is a name worth keeping an eye on.

His story is ultimately being written through his music—one release at a time.

About Manoj Gadarpuriya

Manoj Gadarpuriya is a singer and music artist known for his original releases and collaborations. His recent catalogue includes My City, My State My Pride, KUDI FIRE, Adat Ban Gai, and Built Different. His music continues to expand across digital music platforms as he develops his artistic identity.
