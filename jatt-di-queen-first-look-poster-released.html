Jatt Di Queen First Look Poster Released: Punjabi Film Set to Arrive in Cinemas on February 12, 2027

The excitement around Punjabi cinema is growing with the unveiling of the first-look poster of Jatt Di Queen, an upcoming Punjabi film presented by Starmoon Film Productions and Chakora Productions. The film is scheduled to release in cinemas on 12 February 2027, giving audiences a special reason to celebrate Valentine’s Day on the big screen.

The first-look poster of Jatt Di Queen was recently unveiled, receiving an encouraging response from audiences and Punjabi cinema lovers across social media. The makers have expressed their gratitude to everyone who liked, commented, shared and supported the announcement.

Jatt Di Queen to Release on Valentine's Day 2027

Produced by Prabh Goraya and Vicky Kamboj, Jatt Di Queen is scheduled for a theatrical release on 12 February 2027. With its Valentine’s Day release, the Punjabi movie is positioned as an upcoming cinematic attraction for audiences looking forward to new Punjabi entertainment in 2027.

The film is directed by Arun Kumar Barnwal, with Sumit Bhatt serving as the writer. The project brings together a large creative and acting team from the Punjabi film industry.

Star-Studded Cast of Jatt Di Queen

The film features King B Chouhan, Rumman Ahmad, Malwai Partap, Sammy Gill, Honey Mattu, Neha Dayal, Pahulpreet Singh, Lucky Kalsi, Deush Mehra and Sukhdev Barnala in the cast. The combination of established and emerging performers is expected to be one of the highlights of the upcoming Punjabi film.

Strong Technical and Creative Team

The cinematography of Jatt Di Queen is handled by Harnek Singh and Rah Sharma, while Rohit Dhiman is the editor. Ajay Maurya serves as Executive Producer.

The film's title song has been edited by Gurjeet Singh and choreographed by Monika Rani. Deush Mehra is also credited as choreographer, while Jasdip Singh serves as Assistant Director.

The technical crew includes Harshdeep Singh as Art Director, Vishal as Camera Operator, Jaskaran Chogawan as DIT, Sharandeep Singh as Gaffer Assistant, Manpreet Singh as Focus Puller and Kuldeep Singh as Gaffer.

Music and Songs Add to the Film's Appeal

Music is another important part of Jatt Di Queen. The film's music directors include Jay K, Goldboy, Amber Vashisht and Kerryy.

The lyric-writing team features Happy Raikoti, Youngveer, Amber Vashisht, Abeer and Sufraaz. Malwai Partap is credited as a playback singer, while Manoj Gadarpuriya and Sachin Dahar are among the singers associated with the project.

Post-production is being handled by Rabab Studio, with AME Digital serving as the Digital Partner.

Poster stills are credited to K Raj Famous Films, while the poster has been designed by Manoj Zore.

Jatt Di Queen Release Date

With the first-look poster now unveiled, anticipation for Jatt Di Queen is expected to continue building ahead of its theatrical release.

The Punjabi movie Jatt Di Queen will release in cinemas on 12 February 2027, just ahead of Valentine’s Day. The makers have urged audiences to continue showing their love and support as the film's journey officially begins.
