Kamz Inkzone: From Starting Music in 2017 to Building His Own Music Production Company Inkboy Musik

Kamaljit Singh, popularly known by his artist name Kamz Inkzone, has built a diverse journey in the Punjabi music industry, evolving from an aspiring music artist into a music producer, songwriter, DJ and music video creator. His journey began in 2017, and over the years he has worked across multiple areas of music, including Punjabi songs, hip-hop, DJ music, remixes and commercial music.

What started with his first track “Ink You” gradually developed into a wider career behind and in front of the camera.

Kamz Inkzone Started His Music Journey in 2017

Kamz Inkzone entered the music space in 2017 with his first track, “Ink You.” During the early phase of his career, he also took a major step by investing in a big-budget music video in Dubai, giving him an opportunity to explore the visual and production side of the music industry.

His journey was not limited to performing. Kamz Inkzone gradually started learning the different aspects involved in creating music and music videos, which later became an important part of his career.

Working With Speed Records Helped Shape His Career

One of the significant phases of Kamz Inkzone's career came through his association with Speed Records. During his time working with the company, he gained experience in different areas of music production, including editing, music, lyrics and video production.

This experience allowed him to understand how different elements come together to create a complete music project. With time, the skills he developed made it possible for him to independently work on songs, music production and videos.

Befikra With Ninja Became a Major Milestone

In 2019, Kamz Inkzone worked with Punjabi singer Ninja on the rap-oriented track “Befikra.” The song became an important milestone in his journey and helped bring greater attention to his work in rap music.

The project also demonstrated his growing ability to work with established Punjabi artists while developing his own identity within the industry.

Launching New Artists and the Success of Hunar Sidhu

Following the success of his work, more singers and artists began approaching Kamz Inkzone for music projects. He also became involved in launching new artists.

One of the notable projects from this phase was Hunar Sidhu's “Sade Siro.” The song went on to cross 25 million views, becoming an important milestone for the artist and further bringing attention to Kamz Inkzone's music journey.

Collaboration With Bohemia

Another major moment in Kamz Inkzone's career came when he got the opportunity to feature with Bohemia, widely known as the Rap King of Punjabi rap.

The collaboration became another significant chapter in his musical journey and opened opportunities for him to expand his work beyond the Indian music scene.

International Music and Shows

Kamz Inkzone's work has also taken him outside India. His music journey has included visits to London, Dubai and Europe for shows, private performances and music video projects.

These international experiences have allowed him to work in different environments and explore new sounds and creative approaches.

From Punjabi Music to DJ and Remix Music

After establishing himself in Punjabi music, Kamz Inkzone also explored the world of DJ music. He learned DJing and started creating DJ-oriented songs.

In 2025, he expanded his musical experimentation further by working on Afro music remixes, Punjabi song remixes and original music featuring other artists.

He also began focusing more on songwriting and creating his own lyrics.

Inspired by Shiv Kumar Batalvi and Bulleh Shah

As Kamz Inkzone developed his songwriting skills, literature also became a source of inspiration. He has explored the works of Punjabi literary figures such as Shiv Kumar Batalvi and Bulleh Shah.

This interest has contributed to his efforts to write his own lyrics and create music that combines different influences.

Inkboy Musik: His Own Music Production Company

In 2026, Kamz Inkzone took another major step by establishing his own music video production company, Inkboy Musik.

The company works across different musical formats and has artists from various genres in its catalogue, including Qawwali, hip-hop, DJ remixes and commercial Punjabi songs.

Today, Kamz Inkzone's work extends beyond being an artist. He is involved in music creation, songwriting, production, DJ music and music video production.

From his first track “Ink You” in 2017 to building Inkboy Musik in 2026, Kamz Inkzone's journey reflects his progression across multiple sides of the Punjabi music industry.
